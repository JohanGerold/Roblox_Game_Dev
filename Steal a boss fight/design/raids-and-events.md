# Raids and Events

## Godly raids

- Every **45 minutes**, a raid boss spawns.
- Drops **godly / multiversal-tier characters** (inspired by characters like Yhwach, Rimuru, Anos, Zeno, with changed names).
- Server-wide countdown and announcement so players stay online and YouTubers can plan videos around it.

## Live events (after launch)

- **Admin abuse events**: the developer spawns rares or boosts luck for everyone at scheduled times. These drive big player spikes in Steal a Brainrot.
- **Like goals**: "Guaranteed God egg at 10K likes."
- **Codes** for free rewards; group-join rewards.
- Frequent update tags in the title (`[UPD 3]`).

## V1 note

Raids come after V1. Once there are players, they're one of the strongest return reasons. See [V1 Scope and Build Order](../planning/v1-scope.md).
