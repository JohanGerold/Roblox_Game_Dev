# The Big Egg

The steal mechanic, and the game's link to the "Steal a…" genre.

## The problem it solves

If the boss itself dropped one stealable prize, the strongest player would win it every time (power creep). Instead:

## Personal eggs by damage rank

- Ranks 1–3 by damage: egg rolls **Mythic or higher**.
- Rank 4 to last: egg rolls **Legendary or lower**.
- Must deal a minimum share of damage to count.

## Rules

- **Every player who fought gets a normal egg.** It is personal and **cannot be stolen**. Nobody leaves a boss fight with nothing, so new players aren't discouraged.
- **One giant egg drops in the arena**, with a server-wide announcement. It has the highest chance of rolling Mythic, Secret, God and other top-tier characters.
- **Anyone can grab it.** The carrier must bring it back to their own base.
- **Carrying it equalizes players:**
  - the carrier is slowed,
  - the carrier glows and shows on everyone's map,
  - the carrier **cannot use stands or speed upgrades** while carrying.
  This makes the chase about skill and teamwork, not level.
- **One hit makes the carrier drop it.** Whoever picks it up next becomes the target, like hot potato, until someone reaches their base.
- **Optional:** the top damage dealers on the boss get a short head start on the egg, so fighting well matters.

## Decided for V1 (2026-10-10)

- Carrier leaves, dies or falls off the map: the egg drops where they last stood on solid ground and can be grabbed again.
- Time limit: the egg returns to the arena once after 3 minutes and disappears after 5 minutes.
- No special escort rules: anyone can hit the carrier, allies included.
- Each boss kill drops its own giant egg, up to 3 at the same time.
