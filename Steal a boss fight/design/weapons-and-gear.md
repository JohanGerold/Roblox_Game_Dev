# Weapons and Gear

## Weapons (bought with money)

- Range from a **stick** up to **OP anime-style weapons**.
- Types (the user is designing these): katana, hammer, daggers, naginata, staff, chains, cards, gravity magic, and more.
- Both **physical and magical**.
- Each weapon has its **own unique moveset**.
- Purpose: drops are luck-based, so a player who never gets a boss moveset still has a strong way to fight.

## Boss armor / clothing (dropped by bosses)

- Normal stage bosses have a **medium chance** to drop pieces of their armor/clothing.
- Wearing it **boosts the player's power**.

## Boss movesets (dropped by bosses)

- **Low chance** to drop. Each copy unlocks one more move. See [Boss Abilities and Copies](boss-abilities-and-copies.md).

## Placeholders

Until real assets exist, weapons and armor use simple placeholder parts and colours.
