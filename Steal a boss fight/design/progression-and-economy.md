# Progression and Economy

## Systems

- **Money per second** from stands placed at your base.
- **Boxing sandbags** to train power.
- **Treadmill** to train speed (from Steal An Egg). Speed matters for chasing or carrying the [giant egg](big-egg.md) home, but carrying cancels speed upgrades.
- **Base upgrades**: more stand slots.
- **Stages**: each new stage is a different anime world with a harder boss.
- **Rebirth**: reset for permanent bonuses; also unlocks extra boss-ability slots (see [Boss Abilities and Copies](boss-abilities-and-copies.md)).
- **Offline earnings (V1)**: money/s × time away, capped about 8 h, about 50% rate.
- **Day/night cycle**: planned. Ideas still open (night-only bosses? stronger drops at night?).

## Big numbers

- Exponential growth with suffixes (K, M, B, T…).
- Large counters, floating "+$1.2M" popups, satisfying sounds.
- Start small (about 1/s) so the first session climbs into thousands or millions; save the huge suffixes for long-term players.

## Rough curve (from research, to tune later)

- Each tier's cost about 2–2.5× the previous.
- Income about 1.7–2× per tier.
- First rebirth after about 25–40 minutes for an engaged new player.

## Monetization ideas (add last)

- 2× Money pass, 2× Hatch Speed pass
- Extra boss-ability slot pass
- Luck boosts, cosmetics/auras
- Show odds for anything random bought with Robux.
