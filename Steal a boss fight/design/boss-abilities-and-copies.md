# Boss Abilities and Copies

## Decision

Players **do not transform into the boss**. Instead, each copy of a boss they collect unlocks more of that boss's abilities on their own character.

Why: playing *as* the boss needs a separate character rig, animation set and camera setup per boss. Abilities are just skills on the player's own character, so each new boss costs a few moves, not a whole character.

## How copies drop

- Every boss kill has a chance to drop a copy of that boss.
- Repeatable: farm the same boss for more copies.

## Unlock ladder (draft numbers)

| Copies | Unlock |
|---|---|
| 1 | Passive (e.g. +10% damage, small aura) |
| 3 | Skill 1 |
| 10 | Skill 2 |
| 25 | Ultimate |
| 50 | Full mastery: the boss's outfit/aura on your avatar + server announcement |

Full mastery keeps the "I became the villain" fantasy for YouTube without the cost of a playable boss.

## Loadout rule (decided)

- You can equip **one boss "set" at a time**.
- **More slots unlock through rebirth or a game pass.**
- This keeps collecting different bosses meaningful.
