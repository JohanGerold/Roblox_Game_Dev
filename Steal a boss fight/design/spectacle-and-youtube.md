# Spectacle and YouTube

## The design rule

**Every few minutes the game should produce a moment worth a video or thumbnail.**

## Why

Roblox's main audience is kids. They want big numbers, cool powers and flashy effects. YouTubers make videos of exactly that (sometimes spending Robux, sometimes given free items/admin by the developer), and kids who watch want to play.

## Clip-worthy moments in this game

- Giant boss fights with big effects
- The server-wide scramble for the [giant egg](big-egg.md)
- Hatching a God / Secret character with a server announcement
- Reaching full mastery of a boss and wearing its aura ([Boss Abilities and Copies](boss-abilities-and-copies.md))
- Godly raids every 45 minutes ([Raids and Events](raids-and-events.md))
- Getting your giant egg stolen one step from home

## Thumbnail ideas

- Avatar holding a giant glowing egg while a huge boss chases them
- Base full of giant mutated stands
- Player with a boss's full-mastery aura and a huge money number

## Watch out for

- **Phones**: most kids play on mobile. Effects must *look* expensive without lagging.
- **Reaching YouTubers**: later, contact small and mid-size Roblox YouTubers and offer free items or admin access.
