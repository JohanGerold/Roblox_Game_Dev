# Stands

Characters hatched from eggs. Inspired by stands from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.

## What they do

- **Fight beside you automatically** during boss fights while you focus on dodging.
- **Earn money per second** when placed at your base.

## At your base

- Stands **shadowbox** at your base (idle animation) while generating money.
- **Upgrade** a stand to raise its money per second and its combat power.

## Rarities

12 tiers from Common to Absolute, with auras from Mythic up and forms as separate characters. See [Rarities and Auras](rarities-and-auras.md). The [giant egg](big-egg.md) has the best odds for the top tiers; [godly raids](raids-and-events.md) drop multiversal-tier characters.

## Spectacle

- Size variants (giant versions) and mutations (Gold, Rainbow, Galaxy…) so a rare stand looks amazing in a thumbnail. See [Spectacle and YouTube](spectacle-and-youtube.md).

## Clean split with boss abilities

- **Stands** = characters you hatch; they fight *for* you.
- **Boss abilities** = skills *you* use, unlocked by boss copies. See [Boss Abilities and Copies](boss-abilities-and-copies.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Roblox requires showing the odds on anything bought with Robux that has a random result. Plan the odds display from the start.
