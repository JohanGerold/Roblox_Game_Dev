# Game Concept

## One-line pitch

**Steal An Egg, but you fight giant anime bosses for the eggs.**

## Theme

Anime. Characters are anime-inspired with changed names (e.g. "Lofy"), the way many Roblox anime games do it. See the IP decision in [Decisions Log](../planning/decisions.md#1-anime-characters-with-changed-names).

## Core loop

1. Go to a stage and fight its huge villain boss together with the server.
2. Your hatched characters ([Stands](stands.md)) fight beside you while you dodge.
3. Boss dies → **everyone gets a normal egg** (personal, cannot be stolen).
4. **One giant egg** also drops in the arena. Anyone can grab it and try to carry it home while others try to steal it. See [The Big Egg](big-egg.md).
5. Eggs hatch at your base into characters that earn money per second and fight as stands.
6. Each boss kill has a chance to drop a **copy of the boss**. Copies unlock that boss's abilities for you. See [Boss Abilities and Copies](boss-abilities-and-copies.md).
7. Spend money on speed (treadmill), base upgrades and harder stages; rebirth for permanent bonuses. See [Progression and Economy](progression-and-economy.md).
8. Every 45 minutes a raid spawns a godly boss with the best drops. See [Raids and Events](raids-and-events.md).
9. Repeat with the next stage, each a different anime world.

## Why it should work

- It keeps the proven Steal An Egg loop (grab, escape, hatch, earn, upgrade).
- It adds what kids watch YouTubers for: huge bosses, cool powers, big numbers, flashy effects. See [Spectacle and YouTube](spectacle-and-youtube.md).
- The giant egg scramble after every boss is a repeatable, clip-worthy moment.
