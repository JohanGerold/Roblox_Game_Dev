# Steal a Boss Fight — Index

Working title. A Roblox game: fight huge anime-style raid bosses, everyone gets an egg, one giant egg is up for grabs and can be stolen on the way home. Hatched characters fight beside you like stands, and copies of a boss unlock its abilities for you.
