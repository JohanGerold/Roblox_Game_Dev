# V1 Scope and Build Order

## Principle

Make one stage feel great and get testers coming back. Everything else stacks on top. Building everything at once is how solo projects stall.

## V1 contents

| Keep | Simplified for V1 | Later |
|---|---|---|
| One huge boss with big effects | 1 stage, 1 boss | More stages / anime worlds |
| Normal eggs + giant stealable egg | About 6 hatchable characters | Godly raids every 45 min |
| Copies unlock boss abilities | Stands auto-fight; one generous dodge | Parry, perfect-dodge |
| Big numbers, money per second | Treadmill + simple rebirth | Day/night, PvP arena |
| Saving progress | | Monetization (added last) |

## Build order

1. **Graybox**: 8 player bases, one arena, one boss with a simple attack, a player dodge.
2. **Eggs**: boss dies → everyone gets an egg → hatch at base → stand earns money. Save data reliably.
3. **Giant egg**: drop, pick up, carry rules, hit-to-drop, deliver to base.
4. **Stands in combat**: hatched stand follows you and attacks the boss.
5. **Boss copies**: drop chance and the first two unlock steps (passive, skill 1).
6. **Treadmill and rebirth**.
7. **Playtest** with friends: is the first 10 minutes clear and fun? Try to exploit it.
8. **Polish the spectacle**: effects, sounds, number popups, announcements.
9. **Add monetization** at natural moments.
10. **Launch**, then add raids, events, codes and like goals.

> [!NOTE]
> With zero experience, do the [Learning Path](learning-path.md) first. Each build step above will take longer than a skilled developer would need, and that's fine.
